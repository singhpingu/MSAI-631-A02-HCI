How this study space adapts

Guidance changes. Guided view expands an example and hint. Focused view starts with the concept collapsed. The question, answers, and navigation remain available in every view.

Your choice comes first. Adaptation starts off. Disable it to stop collecting new observations. Reset clears the session. Reloading this page also clears all history.

A demonstration model. Logistic regression was trained offline on 240 synthetic profiles. It is not a measure of your ability, and its score is not a validated estimate of your learning needs.
